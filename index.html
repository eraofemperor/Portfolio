Methodology

How does Akhil turn raw data into decisions?

Every project follows the same eight steps, from business question to recommendation.

  1. 01 · UNDERSTAND
    Business Objective
    Clarify stakeholder KPIs, decision bottlenecks and success criteria.
  2. 02 · INSPECT
    Schema & Quality
    Audit raw schemas, data types, cardinality, nulls and duplicates.
  3. 03 · CLEAN
    Validation
    Apply validation rules, impute or drop invalid records, isolate outliers.
  4. 04 · TRANSFORM
    Feature Engineering
    Build calculated dimensions, time lags and star schemas.
  5. 05 · ANALYZE
    SQL & Modeling
    Run multi-level aggregations, window ranks and variance checks.
  6. 06 · VISUALIZE
    BI Dashboarding
    Build dashboards with slicers and drill-downs.
  7. 07 · DIAGNOSE
    Root Cause
    Separate seasonal noise from structural shifts.
  8. 08 · ACT
    Recommendation
    Turn findings into prioritised operational decisions.
