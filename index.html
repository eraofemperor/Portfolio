Methodology

The 8-Stage Analytical Pipeline

How every business question moves from raw, unstructured data into verified executive action.

01 · UNDERSTAND
Business Objective
Clarify stakeholder KPIs, core decision bottlenecks, and measurable success criteria.
02 · INSPECT
Schema & Quality
Audit raw schemas, data types, cardinality, missing null distribution, and duplicates.
03 · CLEAN
Validation Hygiene
Apply programmatic validation rules, impute or drop invalid records, and isolate outliers.
04 · TRANSFORM
Feature Engineering
Construct calculated dimensions, time lags, moving averages, and relational star schemas.
05 · ANALYZE
SQL & Modeling
Execute multi-level aggregations, window partition ranks, and statistical variance checks.
06 · VISUALIZE
BI Dashboarding
Build intuitive visual representations with dynamic slicers and drill-down capabilities.
07 · DIAGNOSE
Root Cause Insight
Synthesize why anomalies occurred—separating seasonal noise from structural shifts.
08 · ACT
Recommendation
Translate diagnostic findings into prioritized, high-leverage operational decisions.
