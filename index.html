Methodology

How does Akhil turn raw data into decisions?

Every project follows the same eight steps, from business question to recommendation.

  1. 01 · UNDERSTAND
    Business Objective
    Clarify stakeholder KPIs, decision bottlenecks and success criteria.
  2. 02 · INSPECT
    Schema & Quality
    Audit raw schemas, data types, cardinality, nulls and duplicates.
  3. 03 · CLEAN
    Validation
    Apply validation rules, impute or drop invalid records, isolate outliers.
  4. 04 · TRANSFORM
    Feature Engineering
    Build calculated dimensions, time lags and star schemas.
  5. 05 · ANALYZE
    SQL & Modeling
    Run multi-level aggregations, window ranks and variance checks.
  6. 06 · VISUALIZE
    BI Dashboarding
    Build dashboards with slicers and drill-downs.
  7. 07 · DIAGNOSE
    Root Cause
    Separate seasonal noise from structural shifts.
  8. 08 · ACT
    Recommendation
    Turn findings into prioritised operational decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Akhil Shibu?+

Akhil Shibu is a Data Analyst and Power BI specialist from Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India. He holds an MCA from CHMM College for Advanced Studies (Kerala University, 2026) and builds SQL analyses, Python data-cleaning pipelines and Power BI dashboards that turn raw business records into clear decisions. His portfolio, published under the name Akhil Insights, contains three case studies: a Kerala road accident analysis, the Datasentrix data-cleaning platform and a bike sales Excel dashboard.

What does Akhil build?+

Akhil builds data analytics deliverables: SQL queries and models, automated Python and Django data-cleaning pipelines, Power BI dashboards with DAX measures, and Excel models with pivot tables and slicers. Each project starts with a business question and ends with a KPI view that a manager can act on. The three portfolio projects show this range: public-safety analysis (Power BI and SQL), a validation platform (Python and Django) and a commercial dashboard (Excel).

What is Datasentrix?+

Datasentrix is an automated data hygiene and reporting platform built by Akhil Shibu with Python, Django and SQL. It validates uploaded spreadsheets, drops records with null primary keys, isolates outliers with Z-scores and generates KPI reports, so teams stop repeating manual clean-up before every dashboard. Read the full write-up, including the problem, pipeline stages and tools, on the Datasentrix case study page.
