Bike Sales Excel Dashboard: Margin and Region Analysis

This case study is a multi-sheet Microsoft Excel workbook that answers which bike products, categories and regions drive sales and gross margin. It uses XLOOKUP, dynamic pivot tables, slicer timelines and KPI scorecards so a manager can filter by period, region or customer group in seconds.

Project facts
RoleAnalyst (solo portfolio project)
ToolMicrosoft Excel
TechniquesXLOOKUP, pivot tables, slicers, KPI cards
FocusSales, margin, category and regional trends
Codegithub.com/eraofemperor/Bike-Sales-Excel-Dashboard

What problem does this project solve?

Which products, categories and regional distribution tiers drive sales performance and gross margin, and how do customer demographics change what people buy?

How was it built? (method)

  1. Cleaned the sales data and standardised categories and demographic fields.
  2. Joined lookup tables with XLOOKUP and conditional logic.
  3. Built dynamic pivot tables for product, category and region drill-downs.
  4. Added slicer timelines so any view can be filtered by period.
  5. Summarised results in KPI scorecards for revenue and margin.

What were the results?

MeasureResult
Dashboard viewsSales performance, category and product drill-down
DimensionsRegion, customer demographics, time
InteractivitySlicers and timeline filters
OutputKPI summary scorecards

Why Excel instead of Power BI?

Many small teams already work in Excel. A well-modelled workbook with pivot tables and slicers gives them interactive analysis without new software. For larger data and shared reporting, the same approach moves to Power BI, as in the Kerala road accident analysis.

What makes this a good Excel sales dashboard project?

It uses advanced Excel features recruiters filter for: VLOOKUP and XLOOKUP joins, pivot tables, slicers and an Excel dashboard with KPI cards, all tied to one business question about margin and region.

Frequently asked questions

What does the bike sales Excel dashboard show?+

The workbook shows sales performance, product and category drill-downs, regional and demographic variance, and revenue trends over time. Slicers and a timeline let a user filter every pivot table together, and KPI cards summarise revenue and gross margin at the top.

Which Excel features does the dashboard use?+

It uses XLOOKUP for joining lookup tables, conditional logic for categories, dynamic pivot tables for drill-downs, slicer timelines for filtering and KPI summary scorecards. Together they turn a flat sales table into an interactive dashboard that needs no macros.
