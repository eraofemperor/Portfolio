Methodology

The 8-Stage Analytical Pipeline

How every business question moves from raw, unstructured data into verified executive action.

01 · UNDERSTAND
Business Objective
Clarify stakeholder KPIs, core decision bottlenecks, and measurable success criteria.
02 · INSPECT
Schema & Quality
Audit raw schemas, data types, cardinality, missing null distribution, and duplicates.
03 · CLEAN
Validation Hygiene
Apply programmatic validation rules, impute or drop invalid records, and isolate outliers.
04 · TRANSFORM
Feature Engineering
Construct calculated dimensions, time lags, moving averages, and relational star schemas.
05 · ANALYZE
SQL & Modeling
Execute multi-level aggregations, window partition ranks, and statistical variance checks.
06 · VISUALIZE
BI Dashboarding
Build intuitive visual representations with dynamic slicers and drill-down capabilities.
07 · DIAGNOSE
Root Cause Insight
Synthesize why anomalies occurred—separating seasonal noise from structural shifts.
08 · ACT
Recommendation
Translate diagnostic findings into prioritized, high-leverage operational decisions.

Analytical Terminology & Domain Glossary

Core definitions utilized across portfolio data pipelines, statistical modeling, and executive reporting architectures:

ETL Pipeline
Extract, Transform, Load: Automated architecture that extracts raw tabular records, executes validation sanitization, and loads clean data into analytical repositories.
DAX Measure
Data Analysis Expressions: Formula language in Power BI used to compute dynamic, context-aware aggregations, time intelligence shifts, and KPI variances across star schemas.
Star Schema
Dimensional data modeling standard separating numeric transactional measures (fact tables) from contextual lookup attributes (dimension tables) to optimize query speed.
Z-Score Anomaly Detection
Statistical measurement quantifying how many standard deviations an observation deviates from the mean, isolating operational data errors without skewing trendlines.
Spatial Severity Index
A normalized risk index weighting incident counts by fatality and severe injury metrics to calculate geographical corridor risk profiles independently of population density.
