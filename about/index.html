Who is Akhil Shibu?

I am an MCA graduate from CHMM College for Advanced Studies, specialising in data analytics, business intelligence and automated ETL pipelines. My work targets real business and public-safety questions: where road accidents concentrate, how to clean messy spreadsheets repeatably, and how to give managers a dashboard they can trust.

I cover the full lifecycle: scope the question, profile the raw schema, validate and clean records, aggregate with SQL window functions, and build Power BI star-schema models.

My experience comes from three portfolio projects (see the data analytics case studies) and two virtual job simulations on Forage.

What principles guide the work?

  • Data hygiene first. Audit schema drift, nulls and outliers before drawing conclusions.
  • Decision-centric reporting. Build KPI scorecards that answer a specific management question.
  • Automated and repeatable. Prefer SQL scripts and Python pipelines over manual steps, as in Datasentrix.
